Separate panels

Downloads one DXF per panel type, plus a manifest. The -xN filename suffix tells you how many copies to cut.

Uses the shared case quantity and hole clearance. Stock size, gap, rotation, and saw options do not affect these files.

DXF technical details

Full ZIP: each file uses a configuration-rich name such as sheet-01-6W-5D-4H-6mm-xN.dxf; xN is the number of identical sheets to cut.

Separate ZIP: each role uses the same pattern, such as front-6W-5D-4H-6mm-xN.dxf or bottom-6W-5D-4H-6mm-xN.dxf.

Compatibility: files use AutoCAD R12 (AC1009) ASCII and legacy closed polylines for broad CorelDRAW/CAD compatibility. Choose millimeters if your importer asks for units.

Cut order: in your laser software, cut CUT_HOLES_FIRST before CUT_OUTLINES_LAST. Physical DXFs contain cut geometry only.

Kerf: the 0.10 mm Full-export minimum is only a validation floor, not a universally safe production gap. Inspect compensated toolpaths in your laser/CAM software.
